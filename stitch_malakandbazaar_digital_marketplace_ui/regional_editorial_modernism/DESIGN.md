## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a dignified, high-trust digital trading forum reflecting the topography, heritage, and commercial vitality of Malakand District. Balancing institutional reliability with regional cultural warmth, it rejects cluttered, utilitarian classified-ad aesthetics in favor of a clean, editorial layout.

The visual style blends **Contemporary Editorial Craft** with **Organic Tactility**:
- **Atmosphere & Essence:** Evokes mist over northern valley pines, stone-carved paths, and earthy river terraces. High-grade whitespace conveys legitimacy and safety for transactions involving real estate, motor vehicles, local orchard produce, and artisan wares.
- **Tone:** Grounded, authentic, transparent, and effortlessly premium.
- **Interaction Ethos:** Direct, low-friction mobile actions (direct dial, WhatsApp dispatch, verification badges) paired with serene visual pacing and deliberate hierarchy.

## Colors
The color architecture draws from the high-altitude landscape of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa:

- **Primary Forest Green (`#1F4D3A`):** Anchors authority, core navigation elements, principal CTAs, and verified seller credentials.
- **Secondary Alpine Moss (`#3F6B52`):** Used for supporting status indicators, interactive hover states, active category filters, and secondary button tiers.
- **Tertiary Warm Sand / Earth (`#C89B6D`):** Provides a warm, artisanal accent. Highlights price anchors, premium featured listings, artisan badges, and conversion touchpoints.
- **Soft Mist Canvas (`#F5F6F2`):** The primary background surface, cutting harsh glare and offering an organic, matte paper finish.
- **Dark Forest Charcoal (`#1E2923`):** High-legibility neutral tone replacing pure black across primary typography and iconography.
- **Subtle Sky Mist (`#7FA9B8`):** An auxiliary atmospheric tone used for location pins, regional river tags, active map interfaces, and subtle callout pill backgrounds.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for card containers and floating dialogs to maintain clear layered contrast against the mist canvas.

## Typography
The system uses Plus Jakarta Sans throughout, delivering modern geometric clarity enriched by subtle humanist warmth. Its open counters and geometric structure preserve legibility under direct sunlight on low-cost mobile displays, while high-weight treatments yield editorial presence.

- **Numerics & Pricing:** Tabular figures are enforced across price tags and phone details to maintain vertical alignment across multi-item lists.
- **Bilingual & Transliteration Readiness:** Ample line heights accommodate vertical diacritics and bilingual interfaces (English/Urdu/Pashto labels).
- **Scale Usage:** Headline scales command authority without overcrowding. Micro labels use slightly wide tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.02em - 0.04em`) to maintain clarity in status pills and category markers.

## Layout & Spacing
A responsive fluid grid establishes breathing room and structure across viewport scales:

- **Mobile (320px - 639px):** 4-column layout with `margin: 1rem` (16px) and `gutter: 1rem`. Full-bleed edge gutters ensure listing cards maximize tap targets. Sticky communication footers hug the device safe bottom zone.
- **Tablet (640px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter-md: 1.5rem` (24px). Split-pane browsing pairs visual maps/filters with dual-card listing flows.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1280px` with `margin-lg: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter-lg: 2rem` (32px), positioning search and verified seller credentials side-by-side.

Rhythmic vertical margins prevent visual compression, ensuring Malakand's diverse trade items (vehicles, crops, homes, textiles) inhabit distinct visual registers.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth mimics physical paper and fine stone planes layered over alpine earth, avoiding synthetic artificial drops.

- **Atmospheric Cast Shadows:** Shadows carry a very faint Forest Charcoal tint (`rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.06)` to `rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.12)`) rather than dead neutral gray, giving cards an organic feel on top of the mist canvas.
- **Surface Elevation Steps:**
  - *Base Ground (`#F5F6F2`):* Canvas foundation.
  - *Raised Level (`#FFFFFF`):* Marketplace listing cards, input plates. Soft shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.04)`.
  - *Floating Level:* Sticky bottom action bars, verified trust drawers, dropdown menus. Deep soft shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.04)`.
- **Low-Contrast Separation:** Cards and layered surfaces pair elevation with a fine border (`1px solid rgba(31, 77, 58, 0.08)`), maintaining clear boundaries on low-contrast screens.

## Shapes
A roundedness factor of `2` provides balanced contours throughout:
- Base components (buttons, text inputs, badge pills) adopt `0.5rem` (8px) radius for clean tactile definition.
- Larger structures (listing cards, product image frames, modal containers) leverage `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Status tokens and action tags use fully pill-shaped radiuses to signify interactive touch readiness.
- Topographic mountain motifs and contour watermarks utilize organic, rounded curves to honor the regional geography without impeding typographic grids.

## Components

### Action Buttons & CTA Architecture
- **Primary Action (Direct Dial / Bid):** Background `#1F4D3A`, label `#FFFFFF`, semi-bold typography. Tactile state transition: lifts 1px with enhanced shadow on hover, relaxes to subtle inset on press.
- **WhatsApp Action Highlight:** Background `#25D366` or Deep Green `#1F4D3A` with bright Emerald edge accent. Paired with a phone/chat glyph for rapid one-touch regional inquiry.
- **Secondary Action:** Ghost style, background transparent, border `1.5px solid #3F6B52`, text `#1F4D3A`.
- **Tertiary Accent (Feature Boost / Sand):** Background `#C89B6D`, text `#FFFFFF`, applied selectively to high-priority upgrades.

### Marketplace Cards
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, `16px` inner padding, image container pinned to top with an aspect ratio of 4:3 or 16:9.
- **Metadata Layout:** Includes Tehsil/Subdivision tag (e.g., *Batkhela*, *Dargai*, *Thana*) paired with a Subtle Sky (`#7FA9B8`) map pin icon.
- **Price Anchor:** Emphasized with `headline-sm` in `#1F4D3A` alongside an Earth Sand badge (`#C89B6D`) for verified market-rate indicators.

### Category Chips & Badges
- **Inactive Chip:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E2923`, fine border `1px solid rgba(31, 77, 58, 0.12)`.
- **Active Chip:** Background `#1F4D3A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `#1F4D3A`.
- **Trust Badges:** Rounded pill format, background `#E8EFEA`, icon + label in `#1F4D3A` signifying phone, CNIC, or verified local trader status.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Search Bar:** Prominent, rounded (12px), background `#FFFFFF`, leading magnifying icon in `#3F6B52`. Placeholder text in `#1E2923` at 50% opacity.
- **Fields:** 48px height on mobile to ensure thumb ergonomics. Focused state features a crisp 2px ring in `#1F4D3A` with no harsh outline flash.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Forest green accents (`#1F4D3A`) when checked with soft micro-scaling animation. Inactive borders use `#3F6B52` at 40% opacity on white backdrops.

### Mobile Sticky Utility Bar
- Bottom-docked bar displaying price, instant seller avatar, direct call button, and prominent green WhatsApp chat CTA, ensuring immediate trade access without full-page scrolling.
